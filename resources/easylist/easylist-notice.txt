Dictol bundles curated third-party ad-server domain rules for the embedded
browser:

- Global source reference: https://easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org/easylist.txt
- China source reference: https://easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org/easylistchina.txt

The bundled files are a deliberately small, manually curated subset of common
advertising platforms rather than the complete upstream lists. The upstream
lists are maintained by the EasyList project; see the project licence and
attribution requirements at https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html.

All bundled rules are network-only and third-party scoped. Cosmetic,
extended-CSS, style, and scriptlet rules are not included because Dictol blocks
requests before page elements are available.
